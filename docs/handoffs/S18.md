## What exists now
- Buses (`default_bus_layout.tres`): Master ← World(reverb) ← SFX(low-pass); Music/Ambience (low-pass), Dread, Stingers, UI.
  Remote voices (plain and `Voice_<peer>` FX buses) are re-routed into World by name, so voice gets room echo too.
- `Sfx` (`game/audio/sfx.gd`): `play_3d / play_2d / play_ui / attach_loop / make_3d` by cue NAME. `SoundBank` +
  generated `sound_bank.tres`: 51 cues, 31 from CC0 libraries, 20 synth placeholders (all music/dread stems = AI asks).
- `AudioDirector` (`Systems/Audio`, `[Audio]` logs): room-probe reverb (8 rays, 6 Hz) + occlusion of the nearest 6
  sources; FootstepEmitter on every player (sprint 14 m, walk 7 m, crouch/still 0, jump/land/mantle, mage heavier +
  ember + tier-scaled aura); chicken squawk when a mage is within 8 m; hide-spot creaks; carry, fall thud, hurt/death;
  mage casts detected from `MageAbilities.cooldown_left()` (no edits to game/mage); kill sting, Fire Wave ready, tier-up.
- Dread (`DreadTracker`): local alive villager only, **LOS only**, drone 40 / heartbeat 25 (speeds up) / strings 15 /
  chase after "spotted"; hysteresis + 1.5 s in / 3 s out. Mage gets a hunter bed, dead get a ghost bed.
- Music arc: 3 synced stems (cheer → dark → drone) by `match_dread` (hunt time, storm stage, mage tier); low-pass and
  slight pitch drop at high dread; ducks -7 dB under remote voice. Win/defeat + round-start stingers.
- Storm: roar + crackle slide along the ring at the point nearest you; wind + muffle (SFX/ambience/music, never voice)
  outside the zone; warning bell a whole step up per stage; sweep + rumble per stage; damage tick. Ocean loop as a
  compass, gulls + town bed thin with dread / near the storm, crackle on the nearest 8 torch/candle/lantern props.
- Tuning: `game/audio/tuning_audio.tres` (`--tune=audio.x=y`). `AudioDirector.set_bus_volume_db(bus, db)` for the menu.
