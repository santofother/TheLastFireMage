## What exists now
- `SpectatorManager` (local per peer): on death / role SPECTATOR the player becomes a translucent third-person
  ghost (mouse look, WASD, Shift fast) walking on a one-way glass plane `camera_height` m above the map.
  The glass is back-face culled (invisible from below, living players never see it); a shader fogs it opaque
  dark beyond `vision_radius` around the ghost, clear inside, so only the map under the ghost is visible.
- Exits on `player_revived` / new alive role, restoring player camera and `VoiceManager.listener`.
- While spectating `VoiceManager.listener` = spectator camera (dead keep proximity voice).

## Public API
- `is_spectating`, `enter_spectator()`, `exit_spectator()`, `camera`, `focus`, signal `spectating_changed(on)`.
- Voice group: calls `VoiceManager.set_voice_group(peer, &"dead"/&"alive")` only if it exists (S12).
