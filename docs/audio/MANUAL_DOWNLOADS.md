# Manual downloads (S18)
Files that fit but couldn't be fetched/used automatically. Nothing here needs an account.

| Cue | What / why manual | URL | License |
|---|---|---|---|
| `amb_ocean_loop` | "Beach Ocean Waves" by jasinski (via qubodup): only FLAC on the page, and Godot can't import FLAC and there's no converter on the build Mac. Convert one wave to OGG (e.g. Audacity: Export as OGG, q4, loop-trim) and save as `assets/audio/ambience/amb_ocean_loop.ogg`. | https://opengameart.org/content/beach-ocean-waves | CC0 |
| (all) Kenney packs | kenney.nl returns 403 to scripted downloads, so its packs weren't used directly (its "50 RPG sound effects" came via OpenGameArt). Download "Impact Sounds" / "Interface Sounds" by hand if you want surface-specific footsteps (wood/grass) and nicer UI clicks. | https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds, https://kenney.nl/assets/interface-sounds | CC0 |
